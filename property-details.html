Overview & Architectural Pedigree

Positioned along the most storied promontory of Cap d'Antibes, Villa Solaria represents an uncompromising triumph of modernist Mediterranean architecture. Conceptualized with subtle homage to minimalist sculptural principles, the residence is built from bespoke monolithic Italian travertine blocks and hand-honed Vicenza limestone, framed by ultra-slim insulated bronze joinery.

Engineered with complete acoustic privacy and military-grade biometric perimeter protection, the villa captures panoramic south-facing sun exposure across all three structural levels. Sustainable geothermal heating, discrete solar glass integration, and an automated bioclimatic louvered roof system ensure an effortless balance of ecological stewardship and sovereign luxury.
